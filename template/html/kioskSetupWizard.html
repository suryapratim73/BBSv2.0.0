👤 Customer Selection

Should the kiosk require customer identification during checkout?

Yes — Select Customer

Customers must be identified before completing a sale. Best for businesses with regular clients and credit accounts.

No — Walk-in Sales

Allow anonymous walk-in purchases without customer selection. Best for retail counters and quick-service setups.

📦 Product Layout

How should products be organized on the kiosk screen?

Uncategorized List

All products in a single list with a search field. Simple and straightforward.

Auto-Categorized

Products are automatically grouped by keywords detected in their names. You can refine these categories later using the Catalogue Creator.

Manual Categories

Use categories you have already defined in the Catalogue Creator. Items without a category will appear in an "Uncategorized" section.

🗄️ Database Mode

How should kiosk sales interact with your inventory and accounts?

Order Only

Kiosk sales are saved as pending orders. No stock is deducted and no automatic bills are generated. Best when you want to review orders before processing.

Auto Bill

Full automatic processing: stock quantities are updated, customer ledgers are maintained, and bills are generated immediately. Best for fully automated setups.

🧾 Receipt Generation

Should the kiosk generate and print a receipt for each sale?

Yes — Print Receipt

Generate and print a receipt/coupon for every kiosk sale automatically.

No — Skip Receipt

Skip receipt generation. Sales are recorded in the system without printing.
